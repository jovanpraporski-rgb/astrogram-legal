Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026.

1. Kontrolor i kontakt

Kontrolor podataka za mobilnu aplikaciju Astrogram je , fizičko lice iz Srbije. Kontakt: . Astrogram je namenjen isključivo osobama od 18 ili više godina.

2. Podaci koje obrađujemo

  • Nalog: email, interni korisnički ID, status potvrde emaila, ime ili nadimak i jezik.
  • Astro profil: datum i vreme rođenja, podatak da vreme nije poznato, grad i država rođenja, koordinate i vremenska zona tog istorijskog mesta, rod/pol i interesovanja.
  • Obaveštenja: izbor podsetnika, vremenska zona uređaja, jezik, status dozvole i Firebase registracioni token. Podsetnike zakazujemo oko 09:00 po vremenu uređaja.
  • Dijagnostika rušenja: Crashlytics stack trace, verzija aplikacije i sistema, model uređaja i nasumični instalacioni identifikatori. Ne dodajemo ime, email, astro profil, natalne rezultate ili sadržaj tarota.
  • Opciona analitika: tek uz izbor u Podešavanjima šaljemo osnovni naziv ekrana/funkcije i rezultat uspeh/greška, bez stabilnog ID-ja naloga i privatnih astro vrednosti.
  • Urednički sadržaj: ovlašćeni administratori mogu dobrovoljno poslati slike za članke. Običnim korisnicima ne tražimo pristup fotografijama.

Aplikacija ne koristi GPS dozvolu. Mesto rođenja nije trenutna fizička lokacija korisnika.

3. Svrhe i pravni osnovi

Nalog, profil, čuvanje podešavanja i astro izračuni obrađuju se radi pružanja usluge koju korisnik traži. Opcione notifikacije i anonimna analitika aktiviraju se tek nakon izričitog izbora koji se može povući u Podešavanjima. Tehnički izveštaji o rušenju obrađuju se zbog legitimnog interesa da servis bude bezbedan, pouzdan i kvalitetan. Podatke čuvamo radi zakonske obaveze samo kada je takva obaveza primenljiva.

4. Pružaoci usluga i prenosi

Koristimo Supabase za autentifikaciju, bazu, skladište i serverske funkcije; Firebase Cloud Messaging za opciona push obaveštenja; Firebase Crashlytics za dijagnostiku rušenja; opcionu Firebase Analytics analitiku uz saglasnost; Google Play za distribuciju; i Astrogram astro servis za astro izračune i pretragu mesta. Podaci mogu biti obrađeni na infrastrukturi ovih pružalaca izvan tvoje države, uz njihove ugovorne i tehničke zaštitne mere. Ne prodajemo podatke, ne prikazujemo oglase i ne delimo podatke sa oglašivačima.

5. Čuvanje i bezbednost

Nalog i profil čuvamo dok je nalog aktivan. Firebase Analytics korisnički i događajni podaci podešavaju se na najkraću ponuđenu retenciju od 2 meseca, bez resetovanja roka novom aktivnošću. Firebase Crashlytics čuva stack trace, povezane dijagnostičke podatke i instalacione identifikatore 90 dana pre početka uklanjanja. Firebase token brišemo pri odjavi, brisanju naloga ili kada postane nevažeći. Minimalni zapisi mogu se zadržati duže samo zbog zakonske obaveze, sprečavanja prevare, bezbednosti ili rešavanja spora.

Redovni rok čuvanja rezervnih kopija baze je do 30 dana. Duže čuvanje predviđeno je samo dok je kopija potrebna za aktivan incident.

Koristimo HTTPS, ograničene pristupe, Supabase Row Level Security i odvojene administratorske uloge. Nijedan sistem ne može garantovati apsolutnu bezbednost.

6. Brisanje i prava

U Profilu izaberi „Obriši nalog i podatke“ da odmah obrišeš nalog i povezane podatke iz aktivnog sistema. Bez aplikacije koristi javnu stranicu za brisanje. O postupanju te obaveštavamo bez odlaganja, najkasnije 30 dana od prijema zahteva. Ako je potrebno, zatražićemo potvrdu vlasništva pre brisanja. Rezervne kopije nastale pre brisanja mogu sadržati ranije stanje podataka dok se ne uklone.

Anonimnu analitiku možeš odmah isključiti u Podešavanjima. U skladu sa primenljivim pravom možeš tražiti pristup, ispravku, brisanje, ograničenje, prenosivost, prigovor i povlačenje saglasnosti. Možeš se obratiti i nadležnom organu za zaštitu podataka.

7. Buduće funkcije i izmene

Trenutna verzija nema oglase, naplatu niti AI astrologa. Pre uvođenja Google Play Billing-a, AI obrade ili novog procesora ažuriraćemo ovu politiku, Play deklaraciju i saglasnost kada je potrebna. Materijalne izmene biće jasno saopštene.

1. Controller and contact

The data controller for the Astrogram mobile application is , an individual based in Serbia. Contact: . Astrogram is intended only for people aged 18 or older.

2. Data we process

  • Account: email, internal user ID, email confirmation status, name or nickname, and language.
  • Astrology profile: birth date and time, unknown-time flag, birth city and country, coordinates and time zone of that historical place, gender, and interests.
  • Notifications: reminder choices, device time zone, language, permission status, and Firebase device registration token. Reminders are scheduled for around 9:00 a.m. in the device time zone.
  • Crash diagnostics: Crashlytics stack traces, app and operating-system version, device model, and random installation identifiers. We do not add a name, email, astrology profile, natal results, or tarot content.
  • Optional analytics: only after an affirmative Settings choice, we send a basic screen/feature name and success/failure result, without a stable account ID or private astrology values.
  • Editorial content: authorised administrators may voluntarily upload article images. We do not request photo access from ordinary users.

The app does not request GPS permission. A birth place is not the user's current physical location.

3. Purposes and legal bases

We process account, profile, settings, and astrology calculation data to provide the service requested by the user. Optional notifications and anonymous analytics are enabled only after an affirmative choice that can be withdrawn in Settings. Technical crash reports are processed for our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure, reliable, and useful. We retain data for a legal obligation only when such an obligation applies.

4. Service providers and transfers

We use Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and server functions; Firebase Cloud Messaging for optional push notifications; Firebase Crashlytics for crash diagnostics; optional Firebase Analytics with consent; Google Play for distribution; and the Astrogram astrology service for calculations and birth-place search. Data may be processed on these providers' infrastructure outside your country, subject to their contractual and technical safeguards. We do not sell data, show ads, or share data with advertisers.

5. Retention and security

Account and profile data are retained while the account is active. Firebase Analytics user-level and event-level data are configured to the shortest available two-month retention, without resetting the period on new activity. Firebase Crashlytics keeps stack traces, associated diagnostics, and installation identifiers for 90 days before removal begins. A Firebase token is removed on sign-out, account deletion, or invalidation. Minimal records may be retained longer only for a legal obligation, fraud prevention, security, or resolving a dispute.

The standard retention period for database backups is up to 30 days. Longer retention is provided for only while a backup is needed for an active incident.

We use HTTPS, access restrictions, Supabase Row Level Security, and separated administrator roles. No system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Deletion and your rights

In Profile, choose “Delete account and data” to immediately delete your account and associated data from the live system. Without the app, use the public deletion page. We will inform you of the action taken without undue delay and within 30 days of receiving your request. We may ask you to verify account ownership before deletion. Backups created before deletion may still contain earlier data until the backups are removed.

You can immediately disable anonymous analytics in Settings. Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, object to processing, and withdraw consent. You may also complain to the competent data protection authority.

7. Future features and changes

The current version has no ads, payments, or AI astrologer. Before introducing Google Play Billing, AI processing, or a new processor, we will update this policy, the Play disclosure, and consent where required. Material changes will be communicated clearly.
