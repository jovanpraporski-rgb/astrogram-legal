Šta se briše i rok

Iz aktivnog sistema brišemo nalog, profil, podatke o rođenju, interesovanja, podešavanja, tokene uređaja, istoriju povezanu sa nalogom i korisnički sadržaj koji nije zakonski potrebno čuvati. O postupanju te obaveštavamo bez odlaganja, najkasnije 30 dana od prijema zahteva. Ako je potrebno, zatražićemo potvrdu vlasništva pre brisanja. Ograničeni operativni zapisi brišu se u roku do 90 dana. Rezervne kopije nastale pre brisanja mogu sadržati ranije stanje podataka dok se ne uklone. Rokovi čuvanja kopija opisani su u Politici privatnosti. Minimalni zapis može ostati samo kada zakon, bezbednost, sprečavanje prevare ili spor to zahtevaju.

What is deleted and when

We remove the account, profile, birth details, interests, settings, device tokens, account-linked history, and user content not legally required to be retained from the live system. We will inform you of the action taken without undue delay and within 30 days of receiving your request. We may ask you to verify account ownership before deletion. Limited operational records are deleted within 90 days. Backups created before deletion may still contain earlier data until the backups are removed. See the Privacy Policy for backup retention. A minimal record may remain only when required for law, security, fraud prevention, or a dispute.
